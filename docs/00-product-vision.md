# Product Vision

ZenOps is an internal operational platform for managing the daily
reality around employees and projects.

## V1 goals

-   Individual employee work-day records.
-   Work split by project and time interval.
-   Explicit breaks.
-   Morning/night 12-hour shift support.
-   Projects and project-day operational data.
-   Work categories and activities.
-   Machinery, attachments and operating-hour readings.
-   Vehicles with driver/passenger semantics.
-   Fuel consumption and refuelling.
-   Approval by the leader responsible for the project.
-   Live daily reporting.
-   Monthly employee, project, machinery and fuel reports.
-   Monthly closing/locking for payroll and reward preparation.
-   Audit trail.

## Future modules

The home dashboard must support additional modules without redesigning
the application: - vacation requests, - employee requests, -
announcements, - additional HR/operations functionality.

V1 is not an ERP. Customer CRM, contracts, invoicing and accounting are
out of scope.
